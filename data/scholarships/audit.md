## Rejected non-official or out-of-scope sources

- Reddit results were rejected without use.
- arXiv results were irrelevant and rejected without use.
- University reposts/announcements on `iwate-u.ac.jp`, `u-toyama.ac.jp`, `meiji.ac.jp`, `meijigakuin.ac.jp`, `tohoku.ac.jp`, `saitama-u.ac.jp`, and other school domains were rejected as third-party restatements of foundation rules.
- Scholarship search/database pages, including JASSO/MEXT search pages for unrelated awards, were rejected as secondary or irrelevant.
- Rotary’s overseas-applicant scholarship, district encouragement scholarship, and club-support scholarship were not merged into the domestic undergraduate/master’s/doctoral record because they are separate routes.
- Sato Yo’s spring guideline was not used because the current autumn guideline supersedes it for the open round.
- Kyoritsu’s separate 共立メンテナンス奨学基金 and ヤングスチール awards were not merged into the Foundation’s own named award.

No factual claim in the five scholarship records relies on a rejected source.

## Missing or ambiguous information

### JASSO

- No universal student application window, complete initial document list, school nomination quota, internal deadline, detailed selection scoring, or distinct renewal process is public on the reviewed pages.
- “Scholarships that restrict combination” are not exhaustively named on the public page.

### MEXT

- The umbrella scholarship has multiple categories and recommendation routes. Only current 2027 embassy-recommended undergraduate and research guidelines were fully normalized into category-specific details.
- Country-specific deadlines, tests, fields, and quotas must be checked with the relevant Japanese diplomatic mission.
- Other current categories and university/domestic routes require their own guideline-level records; their terms must not be inferred from the two reviewed categories.

### Rotary Yoneyama

- No 2027 academic-year domestic guideline was found on the official pages as of retrieval.
- Designated-school internal deadlines and Rotary-district interview dates/places vary; scoring weights are not public.

### Sato Yo

- No numeric academic or language score threshold is published.
- University internal submission deadlines differ.
- The guideline’s A/B upload deadline classification should be confirmed with the university when an applicant’s situation is unusual.

### Kyoritsu

- A 2027 schedule exists, but the 2027 detailed guideline is not published.
- The public 2026 page omits required documents, visa/residence status, academic/language thresholds, combination rules, detailed selection stages, and renewal conditions.

## Pages requiring manual recheck

1. JASSO “about” page before any CareerVerse release, because it has no visible last-updated date and school-specific deadlines are external to the page.
2. MEXT umbrella and 2027 landing pages, plus the relevant country’s embassy/consulate page, because the official deadline varies by mission.
3. Rotary download page in August 2026 for a possible 2027 guideline and designated-school list.
4. Sato Yo application page immediately before submission; confirm that the dynamic PDF download still resolves and that no deadline correction has been posted.
5. Kyoritsu recruitment and schedule pages in November 2026, when the site says 2027 recruitment begins; replace all 2026 award terms only when an official 2027 guideline is available.
6. Kyoritsu target-school list immediately before advising an applicant, because schools may be removed after two consecutive years without applications and without advance notice.

## Traceability check

- Each scholarship file contains every requested heading.
- Every positive factual section contains a concise summary, a short official excerpt, official page title, official URL, and retrieval date.
- Missing facts are marked unconfirmed and are not inferred.
- Exact amounts, dates, eligibility conditions, exceptions, and application routes were retained in the original Japanese where material.
- The MEXT record explicitly separates umbrella-level statements from the two fully reviewed category guidelines.
- The Kyoritsu record explicitly isolates the Foundation’s own award from two different awards displayed in the same official table.
- Cleaned raw files retain source URL, retrieval date, headings, and substantive page/PDF text while omitting site navigation, scripts, cookie/UI material, and repeated footer content where extraction allowed.

Conclusion: The dataset is complete within the stated official-source scope. Items not supported by an official page are explicitly marked unconfirmed, and no unsupported statement is presented as fact.
