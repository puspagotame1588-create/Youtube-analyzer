# Rotary Yoneyama Memorial Foundation Scholarship

- Official Japanese name: ロータリー米山記念奨学金（学部・修士・博士課程）
- Organization: 公益財団法人ロータリー米山記念奨学会
- Official URL: https://www.rotary-yoneyama.or.jp/content/uploads/pdf-data/201.pdf
- Retrieved date: 2026-08-01
- Source language: Japanese
- Status: active
- Status note: The scholarship program is active, but the published 2026 academic-year application closed on 2025-10-15. A 2027 academic-year guideline was not published on the reviewed official pages as of retrieval.

## Overview

- Structured summary: A grant-type scholarship for foreign students pursuing degrees at Japanese universities/graduate schools, supported principally by donations from Rotary club members across Japan. This record covers the domestic designated-school route for undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral students; it does not merge the separate overseas-applicant, district encouragement, or club-support programs.
- Official evidence: “勉学、研究を志して日本に在留している外国人留学生に対し奨学金を支給”

## Eligibility

- Structured summary: In April 2026 the applicant had to be enrolled or expected to enroll as a foreign student at a Japanese university/graduate school under MEXT jurisdiction, pursue a Japanese degree, be a regular student, be in an eligible year, be academically excellent with interest in intercultural understanding and communication, be physically and mentally able to sustain study abroad, be born on or after 1981-04-02, and be able to fulfill the Yoneyama scholar obligations.
- Official evidence: “日本の大学・大学院の学位取得を目的”; “非正規学生でない”; “1981年4月2日以降に生まれた者”

## Eligible institutions

- Structured summary: The applicant’s April 2026 campus had to be a school/campus designated by the relevant Rotary district and approved by the Foundation. Eligible schools are MEXT-supervised universities. Evening-only, weekend-only, and distance-education courses are excluded. For a consortium graduate school, the school attended for direct supervision is treated as the enrolled school; a multi-campus university is assessed by the campus actually attended.
- Official evidence: “指定校に2026年4月に在籍、進学、編入”; “平日夜間および土日のみの授業又は通信教育課程…対象外”

## Nationality or residence conditions

- Structured summary: In April of the adoption year the applicant must have nationality other than Japan. Dual nationality not including Japanese nationality and stateless status are treated as non-Japanese. The scholarship is for students living in Japan; applicants must be able to attend the domestic district interview and subsequent Rotary activities.
- Official evidence: “採用年の4月に日本以外の国籍を有する者。” / “日本国内で面接試験”

## Visa/status conditions

- Structured summary: In April of the adoption year the applicant must be in Japan for study/research with 在留資格「留学」, or be a student at a Japanese university who is in Japan with recognition by the Minister of Justice as a refugee or evacuee. A person planning to change status could apply conditionally if the updated residence-card PDF was submitted by 2026-03-25.
- Official evidence: “在留資格「留学」で日本に在留” / “「難民」…「避難民」の認定” / “2026年3月25日まで”

## Academic requirements

- Structured summary: Eligible April 2026 years were undergraduate years 3–4 (medicine/dentistry/veterinary years 5–6), master’s years 1–2, and doctoral years 2–3 (medicine/dentistry/veterinary doctoral years 3–4), with equivalent courses/years accepted. Program length can change the eligible year for three-year master’s, law/professional graduate schools, pharmacy, and certain medical engineering programs. Repeating a year and thereby changing the stated April course/year removes eligibility.
- Official evidence: “学部課程3・4年”; “修士課程1・2年”; “博士課程2・3年” / “留年により…変更となる場合は申込資格を失う。”

## Scholarship amount

- Structured summary: Undergraduate: JPY 100,000 per month. Master’s and doctoral: JPY 140,000 per month. The scholarship is a grant with no repayment obligation under normal conditions.

## Payment period

- Structured summary: For April-entry students, eligible earlier years (undergraduate 3, medicine/dentistry/veterinary 5, master’s 1, doctoral 2, medicine/dentistry/veterinary doctoral 3) receive 2026-04 through 2028-03 (two years); final eligible years receive 2026-04 through 2027-03 (one year). For September/October entrants, periods are shortened: earlier years receive about 1 year 5/6 months through August/September 2027, or 1 year 6 months through September 2027; final years receive 5/6 months through August/September 2026, or six months through September 2026. Course completion ends payment earlier where applicable.
- Official evidence: “4月以外の入学の場合、奨学期間が短くなる。” / table headed “奨学期間開始 / 奨学期間終了”

## Application method

- Structured summary: No individual application to the Foundation was accepted for this route. The applicant submitted forms to the responsible office of the designated school; after internal selection, the school checked and consolidated the documents and registered them through the dedicated web screen. Only one university recommendation was accepted where a student applied to multiple schools.
- Official evidence: “個人による当会への申込書の送付、持参は受付けない。” / “学校担当者…専用WEB画面で申込申請”

## Restrictions and combination with other scholarships

- Structured summary: In principle, other scholarships and similar personal grants cannot be received concurrently, including the JASSO Honors Scholarship. The guideline allows specified exceptions: universal local-government international-student benefits; temporary academic prizes; tuition/admission fee reductions or equivalent school awards; research grants restricted to direct research costs; repayment loans; TA/RA/internship remuneration; and certain exchange-activity payments. Other school awards/research aid may be combined only if the total is under JPY 576,000 annually; JPY 576,000 or more is not allowed. A past Yoneyama recipient may not reapply. Improper concurrent receipt cancels status and requires full repayment for the overlap.
- Official evidence: “留学生受入れ促進プログラム…併給を認めない”; “金額＜57万6千円”; “過去に米山奨学金を受給した者には、応募資格はない。”

## Selection process

- Structured summary: Two stages: designated-school internal selection, followed by Rotary district document review and interview in Japan. All recommended eligible applicants had to attend. Interviews were in principle in Japanese; some districts could add a written test. Provisional results were posted to the designated-school web screen from mid-December through early February, with final school notification by the end of March. Final status required attendance at the April district orientation and signing the pledge.
- Official evidence: “学内選考”; “書類審査・面接選考”; “地区によって筆記試験”; “オリエンテーション…正式に奨学生”

## Renewal or continuation conditions

- Structured summary: This route does not describe a routine renewal beyond the award period. Scholar obligations include attending the sponsoring club’s regular meeting at least monthly, submitting scholar reports twice yearly, giving a speech, participating actively, and observing the pledge. The download page separately lists a “club support scholarship” as an extension system for current scholars, but it is a different route and is not treated here as automatic renewal.
- Official evidence: “例会へ毎月1回以上出席”; “年2回、奨学生レポート”; download page: “クラブ支援奨学金（現役奨学生の延長制度）”

## Important exceptions

- Structured summary: Recognized refugees and evacuees at Japanese universities may qualify under the stated documents/status rules. September/October entry shortens the award. A consortium graduate-school applicant applies through the university where they directly study. Excessive planned absence or leave can make the applicant ineligible; if decided before orientation but disclosed only afterward, adoption can be revoked and paid funds reclaimed. Formal scholarship status begins only after the April orientation and signed pledge.
- Official evidence: “難民…避難民”; “4月以外の入学の場合、奨学期間が短くなる”; “オリエンテーション…確約書”
