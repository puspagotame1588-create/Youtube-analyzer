# Kyoritsu International Foundation Scholarship

- Official Japanese name: 一般財団法人共立国際交流奨学財団奨学金
- Organization: 一般財団法人共立国際交流奨学財団（Kyoritsu International Foundation）
- Official URL: https://kif-org.com/scholarship/application.html
- Retrieved date: 2026-08-01
- Source language: Japanese
- Status: unclear
- Status note: The foundation is active, 2026 scholars are receiving the award, and a 2027 selection schedule is posted. However, the official page still publishes 2026 award terms and does not yet publish a 2027 detailed guideline, so current next-cycle eligibility details are unconfirmed.

## Overview

- Structured summary: The Foundation supports students from Asian countries through three awards. This record is limited to the award named 一般財団法人共立国際交流奨学財団奨学金 and does not merge the separate 共立メンテナンス奨学基金 or ヤングスチール awards. For 2026, the named award supported 25 students at JPY 110,000 monthly for one year.
- Official evidence: “アジア諸国からの留学生に対し、勉学に励むための資金援助” / “月額110,000円” / “25名”

## Eligibility

- Structured summary: Confirmed public conditions for the named 2026 award are: applicant from one of the listed countries; enrollment at an eligible school type; application through a registered target school; and recommendation by the school where the student would be enrolled from April 2026. ## Eligible institutions

- Structured summary: For the Foundation’s named award, eligible school types are graduate schools, universities (including junior colleges), and professional training colleges. The institution must be registered on the Foundation’s target-school list. As of 2026-04-01, that list contained 180 universities/junior colleges and 197 professional schools; Japanese-language schools are listed for the separate 共立メンテナンス award, not for the named Foundation award.
- Official evidence: “大学院、大学（短期大学）、専門学校”; “当財団の奨学金に応募するには、対象校として登録される必要があります。”

## Application method

- Structured summary: Apply through a school registered as a Foundation scholarship target school; individual student applications are not accepted. Only a recommendation from the school where the student is enrolled from April 2026 is accepted for the published 2026 round. Students are instructed to contact their school’s responsible office.

- Structured summary: Confirmed public steps are school recommendation, Foundation selection, and notice of selected students. ## Important exceptions

- Structured summary: The same official recruitment table includes three different awards with different school types, amounts, headcounts, and nationality scope. This record uses only the first column, the Foundation’s own named award. Japanese-language schools are eligible only for the separate 共立メンテナンス award. The target-school list can change: schools with no applications for two consecutive years may be removed without advance notice.
- Official evidence: “2年連続で奨学金にご応募のない場合、奨学金対象校から削除”; “削除校への事前連絡は、一切しておりません。”
