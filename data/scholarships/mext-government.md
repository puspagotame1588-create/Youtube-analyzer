# Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship

- Official Japanese name: 日本政府（文部科学省）奨学金 / 国費外国人留学生制度
- Organization: 文部科学省（MEXT）
- Official URL: https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/koutou/ryugaku/06032818.htm
- Retrieved date: 2026-08-01
- Source language: Japanese
- Status: active
- Scope note: This is an umbrella scheme. The current MEXT page lists 2027 embassy-recommended undergraduate, college-of-technology, specialized-training-college, and research programs. Detailed factual fields below are limited to the two current 2027 guidelines reviewed in full: 学部留学生 and 研究留学生. Requirements for other categories or recommendation routes must not be inferred from these two.

## Overview

- Structured summary: The scheme receives outstanding international students to advance international exchange and friendship, human-resource development, and the internationalization and education/research capacity of Japanese higher education. All procedures go through a Japanese diplomatic mission abroad or a Japanese university/school.
- Official evidence: “国費外国人留学生の手続きは全て、在外日本国大使館又は日本国内の大学等を通じて行われます。”
- Page title: 国費外国人留学生制度について

## Eligibility

- Structured summary: Category-specific. For 2027 embassy-recommended undergraduates, applicants must satisfy nationality, age, academic-background, Japanese-learning, health, arrival, and visa conditions. For 2027 embassy-recommended research students, applicants must satisfy nationality, age, graduate-entry academic background, Japanese-learning/adaptation, health, arrival, visa, and field-alignment conditions and must not fall within the listed exclusions.
- Official evidence: Both guidelines state “以下の資格・条件を満たす外国人留学生を募集する。”

## Eligible institutions

- Structured summary: Undergraduate awardees study at Japanese national universities, normally after one year of MEXT-designated preparatory education unless directly placed. Research students conduct research as regular or non-regular students at Japanese universities, graduate schools, or affiliated research institutes; their field must be the field studied at university or a related field that can be researched in a Japanese graduate school.
- Official evidence: Undergraduate: “日本の大学（国立大学）において”; research: “日本の大学において、正規生又は非正規生として研究” and “日本の大学院で研究が可能な分野”

## Nationality or residence conditions

- Structured summary: For both reviewed categories, applicants must have the nationality of a country that has diplomatic relations with Japan. A Japanese national is generally ineligible. A dual national with a base of life outside Japan may qualify only if they plan to choose the foreign nationality and relinquish Japanese nationality by the time of arrival/status activation. The first screening occurs at the diplomatic mission in the country of the nationality chosen by the applicant.
- Official evidence: “日本政府と国交のある国の国籍を有すること。” / “申請時に日本国籍を有する者は原則として募集の対象とならない。”
- Page title: 2027年度 日本政府（文部科学省）奨学金留学生募集要項 学部留学生（大使館推薦）; 研究留学生（大使館推薦）

## Visa/status conditions

- Structured summary: Before travel, an awardee must in principle newly obtain a 「留学」 visa at the Japanese diplomatic mission in their country/region of nationality and enter Japan with the newly acquired 「留学」 residence status. A person already exceptionally in Japan must change or renew to 「留学」 by the end of the month before scholarship payments are due to begin.
- Official evidence: “「留学」の査証を新規取得し、新規取得した「留学」の在留資格で入国すること。”

## Academic requirements

- Structured summary: Undergraduate: born on or after 2002-04-02 in principle and must meet one of the stated Japanese university-entry equivalents, including completion (or qualifying expected completion) of 12 years of schooling, completion of a foreign course corresponding to Japanese upper secondary school, an equivalent qualification examination, or another Japanese university-entry qualification. Research: born on or after 1992-04-02 in principle and must meet the entry qualification for the intended master’s/doctoral route; the guideline gives route-specific 16-year, degree, research-experience, and 18-year criteria. Personal circumstances are not accepted as age exceptions.
- Official evidence: Undergraduate “原則として2002年４月２日以降に出生した者”; research “原則として1992年４月２日以降に出生した者”; undergraduate “学校教育における12年の課程”; research “日本の大学院…入学資格を有する者”

## Language requirements

- Structured summary: Undergraduate applicants must be willing to learn Japanese, deepen their understanding of Japan, and in principle receive university education in Japanese. Research applicants must be willing to learn Japanese, deepen their understanding of Japan, and be able to conduct research and adapt to life in Japan. Research first screening includes written Japanese and English tests; both are required, although the English written test may be waived in an English-official-language country according to the diplomatic mission’s instructions. Undergraduate written subjects depend on the selected humanities/science stream. No universal JLPT/IELTS cutoff is stated in either guideline.
- Official evidence: “積極的に日本語を学習しようとする意欲” / research first screening: “語学筆記試験は日本語及び英語”

## Scholarship amount

- Structured summary: Undergraduate: JPY 117,000 monthly. Research: preparatory education and non-regular students JPY 143,000 monthly; master’s and professional degree courses JPY 144,000 monthly; doctoral courses JPY 145,000 monthly. A JPY 2,000 or JPY 3,000 monthly regional supplement applies in specified areas. Amounts may change each fiscal year depending on the Japanese government budget. Admission, tuition, and entrance-examination fees are not collected, subject to the research guideline’s exception for an examination fee when the student does not advance as a regular student or fails the examination.
- Official evidence: “月額117,000円”; “月額143,000円” / “月額144,000円” / “月額145,000円”; “月額2,000円又は3,000円…加算”
- Page title: 2027年度 日本政府（文部科学省）奨学金留学生募集要項 学部留学生（大使館推薦）; 研究留学生（大使館推薦）

## Payment period

- Structured summary: Undergraduate with preparatory education: 2027-04 through 2032-03 (five years), or through 2034-03 (seven years) for medicine, dentistry, veterinary medicine, or six-year pharmacy. Direct placement: four years, or six years for those fields, beginning at the university-designated intake. Research non-regular: 2027-04 through 2029-03 for April arrival, or 2027-09/10 through 2029-03 for autumn arrival, with the first six months used for preparatory education where required. Research regular students: the standard period needed to complete the course, plus six months of preparatory education only where the university judges it necessary. No payment is made for a month in which status begins after the first day.
- Official evidence: Undergraduate “2027年４月から2032年３月までの５年間”; research “2027年４月から2029年３月まで”; “月の途中で学期が開始…当該月の奨学金は支給しない。”

## Application period and deadline

- Structured summary: There is no single global deadline. Applicants submit to the Japanese diplomatic mission in their country of nationality by that mission’s designated deadline. The research guideline shows recruitment beginning around mid-April 2026 and first screening from early May through around late July; exact submission and screening dates vary by mission. The undergraduate guideline likewise directs applicants to the mission’s designated deadline and gives no universal date.
- Official evidence: “提出期限は在外公館により異なるため、必ず在外公館ホームページ等で確認” / “在外公館にその指定する期限までに提出”
- Page title: 2027年度 日本政府（文部科学省）奨学金留学生募集要項 研究留学生（大使館推薦）; 学部留学生（大使館推薦）

## Application method

- Structured summary: Embassy-recommendation applicants submit the required documents to the Japanese diplomatic mission in their country of nationality. MEXT’s umbrella page also states that all scheme procedures pass through a diplomatic mission or a Japanese university/school, but current university-recommendation details were not generalized from the reviewed embassy guidelines. Application is free.
- Official evidence: “申請者は…国籍国内の在外公館に…提出する。” / “申請は無料です。”

## Required documents

- Structured summary: Undergraduate mandatory set: 2027 application form; all-year academic transcripts from the last school(s); graduation/expected-graduation certificate; recommendation from the homeroom teacher or head of the last school; and 2027 health certificate. Conditional documents include the direct-placement request, current enrollment certificate, university-entry qualification test certificate, and recent language certificate. Research mandatory set: 2027 application form; placement preference form; field of study and research plan; university transcript(s); graduation/completion or degree certificate; recommendation from the current/last university head or academic adviser; and 2027 health certificate. Conditional research documents include thesis abstracts, recent language certificates, employer recommendation, and art/music work evidence. Documents must be Japanese or English or include a Japanese/English translation.
- Official evidence: The undergraduate table marks items ① and ③–⑥ mandatory; the research table marks ①–⑦ mandatory and states “全ての書類は、日本語又は英語…他の言語の場合は…訳文を必ず添付”.

## Restrictions and combination with other scholarships

- Structured summary: Applicants may not make overlapping applications to other MEXT scholarship programs described by the guidelines. A person planning to receive another scholarship/fellowship funded by the Japanese government or related agencies after the MEXT payment period begins is excluded; receiving a non-combinable award after adoption can stop the MEXT award. Prior MEXT participation and current/planned privately financed enrollment in Japan are also disqualifying in specified circumstances, subject to the detailed exceptions in the research guideline. The JASSO Honors Scholarship is expressly not treated as a prior MEXT Scholarship for the prior-award exclusion.
- Official evidence: “他のプログラムとの重複申請” / “併給が認められていない奨学金…支給を受けたとき” / “学習奨励費…は日本政府（文部科学省）奨学金に含まれない。”

## Selection process

- Structured summary: Undergraduate first screening by the diplomatic mission consists of document review, stream-specific written examinations, and interview; MEXT then conducts second screening and selects awardees. Final results are notified through the diplomatic mission approximately from February 2027. Research first screening consists of document review, written Japanese and English tests, and interview. Successful research candidates then request provisional acceptance from Japanese universities by 2026-09-01, submit the placement preference form on the mission’s schedule around mid-September to early October, and undergo MEXT second screening and placement from November; results are notified through the mission from January through March 2027.
- Official evidence: Undergraduate “書類審査、筆記試験及び面接試験”; research “第２次選考・大学配置” and “2027年１月～３月…通知”
- Page title: 2027年度 日本政府（文部科学省）奨学金留学生募集要項 学部留学生（大使館推薦）; 研究留学生（大使館推薦）

## Renewal or continuation conditions

- Structured summary: Undergraduate awardees admitted to a regular graduate course after undergraduate study may apply for an extension; only particularly high-performing students who meet criteria and pass MEXT’s extension review may receive one. Research students progressing from non-regular to regular status or from master’s/professional to doctoral study may apply for extension; they must be approved in the extension review and pass the intended course entrance examination. Non-regular status itself cannot be extended, and advancing without prior extension approval does not extend the award.
- Official evidence: “特に成績優秀な者…延長審査”; research “延長申請に採用され、かつ…試験に合格” and “非正規生としての…延長することはできない。”

## Important exceptions

- Structured summary: Direct-placement undergraduates can begin in April, September, or October and skip preparatory education; university language requirements and possible interviews then apply. Medicine, dentistry, welfare, and related research students cannot undertake clinical training until licensed by the Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare. Specified age exceptions are limited to national-system circumstances such as military service or loss of education due to conflict, not personal circumstances. Force majeure may change travel, cancel the scholarship, or alter the guidelines. Payments can be stopped for false documents, pledge/law violations, disciplinary action, inability to complete in standard time, visa/status changes, unapproved combination, or unauthorized progression.
- Official evidence: “直接配置”; “許可を得るまでは…臨床研修に従事できない”; “個人的事情…一切認めない”; “不可抗力…内容が変更されることがある。”

- Country-specific available fields, quotas, test schedules, and mission procedures are unconfirmed.
