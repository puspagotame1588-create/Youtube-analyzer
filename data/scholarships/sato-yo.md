# Sato Yo International Scholarship Foundation Scholarship

- Official Japanese name: 公益財団法人佐藤陽国際奨学財団 私費留学奨学生
- Organization: 公益財団法人佐藤陽国際奨学財団
- Official URL: https://sisf.or.jp/ja/about_application_autumn/
- Retrieved date: 2026-08-01
- Source language: Japanese
- Status: active
- Status note: The 2026 autumn recruitment, covering 2026-10 and 2027-04 starts, was open on the retrieval date. The applicant form deadline is 2026-08-20 (Thursday) at noon.

## Overview

- Structured summary: A grant for privately financed undergraduate and graduate students from 18 Southeast and Southwest Asian countries, with emphasis on academic excellence, intercultural exchange, social contribution, and applying learning for society. The current autumn round covers October 2026 and April 2027 award starts.
- Official evidence: “2026年10月期 私費留学奨学生 / 2027年4月期 私費留学奨学生” and “学んだことを社会に役立てる意欲のある者”

## Eligibility

- Structured summary: Undergraduate applicants must already attend a Japanese university and be in at least year two at application, or have a confirmed transfer. Graduate applicants must attend a Japanese graduate school or have confirmed admission. All 13 listed qualification conditions must be met; reapplication is allowed. Students beyond the shortest standard program length due to repeating a year are excluded.
- Official evidence: “応募時に2年生以上”; “入学が決定している者”; “以下の条件をすべて満たしていること”; “留年…対象外”

## Eligible institutions

- Structured summary: The student must be a privately financed international student at a university under MEXT jurisdiction. Junior-college students and research students are excluded. New entrants/transfers must attach proof of acceptance; without it they cannot apply. The university’s international-student office participates in the submission route.
- Official evidence: “文部科学省所轄大学に在籍”; “短期大学生及び研究生を除く”; “合格証明書…添付できない場合、応募できません。”

## Nationality or residence conditions

- Structured summary: The applicant may not hold Japanese nationality or nationality outside the listed countries. Eligible countries are Indonesia, Cambodia, Singapore, Thailand, Philippines, Brunei, Vietnam, Malaysia, Myanmar, Laos, Timor-Leste, India, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, and Maldives. The applicant must reside in Japan at the time of application and must not have a parent employed in Japan.
- Official evidence: “日本国及び下記対象国以外の国籍を有していないこと”; “応募時に日本に居住”; “日本で就業している親がいない”

## Academic requirements

- Structured summary: Applicants must be committed to and capable of their studies/research, must not already hold a doctoral degree, and must have at least one year remaining in the course after payments begin. The application requires the full academic-record set specified by level: undergraduates submit high-school and all university records (plus Japanese-language-school records if attended and EJU evidence if available); graduate students submit records from undergraduate year one through the latest available term. No numeric GPA cutoff is published.
- Official evidence: “「博士」の学位を取得していない”; “課程修学期間が1年以上”; “学部1年から直近まで全ての学業成績表”

The essay is handwritten in Japanese; graduate research achievements and the one-page research plan are also submitted in Japanese. No JLPT or score threshold is stated.

## Scholarship amount

- Structured summary: Undergraduate: JPY 180,000 monthly. Graduate: JPY 200,000 monthly. Payment is made every two months, two months at a time, to an account in the scholar’s own name. Additional support: tuition/fee subsidy up to JPY 500,000 per semester with no carryover; graduate conference-attendance subsidy up to JPY 200,000 annually with carryover, halved when Foundation enrollment is under one year.
- Official evidence: “学部生 月額18万円”; “院生 月額20万円”; “半期50万円まで”; “年間20万円を上限”
- Page title: 私費留学奨学生 募集要項（秋募集）

## Payment period

- Structured summary: Two years: October 2026 awardees receive 2026-10 through 2028-09; April 2027 awardees receive 2027-04 through 2029-03. If the standard course period ends within two years, payment ends then. For undergraduates with more than two but less than three years remaining at adoption, support continues through the standard course end. Doctoral students can extend through the standard course period only after passing an extension review approximately 18 months after adoption.
- Official evidence: “支給期間2年間”; “2026年10月から2028年9月”; “2027年4月から2029年3月”

Each university sets its own earlier internal document deadline.
- Official evidence: “応募フォームの締切日 2026年8月20日（木）正午”; “A:2026年8月28日（金） B:2026年9月15日（火）正午”

## Application method

- Structured summary: The student first gets application approval and a management number from the destination university’s international-student office, asks the supervisor for the recommendation, submits the Foundation web form to obtain a receipt number, prepares/scans the documents, and sends them to the university office. The university consolidates and uploads them to SISF. If the destination university cannot process the application, a Japanese institution attended in July 2026 may serve as the application window.
- Official evidence: “進学先大学の留学生課から応募の承諾を得て管理番号を取得”; “留学生課にて提出書類を取りまとめアップロード”

## Required documents

- Structured summary: Resume 1 (Form 1); resume 2 (Form 2); handwritten Japanese essay (Form 3, two A4 pages); supervisor recommendation (Form 4); doctoral evaluation (Form 5, doctoral only); academic transcripts; research achievements (graduate only); one-page Japanese research plan (graduate only); and enrollment certificate, or admission certificate/notice for new entrants. Forms 1–3 must be handwritten in black ballpoint pen. All submission pages must bear the Foundation receipt number; successful applicants later submit originals.
- Official evidence: Official table lists “履歴書1…在学証明書（合格証明書）”; “様式1～3は黒のボールペンで自筆”

## Restrictions and combination with other scholarships

- Structured summary: At the SISF payment start, the student may not receive another scholarship or similar money, salary, or unrestricted money from a special university program. TA/RA remuneration and repayable scholarships are allowed. A current award/program does not bar application if it ends before SISF payments begin. Government-, public-, and company-funded students are excluded.
- Official evidence: “他の機関から奨学金…給与等を受給していない”; “TA/RAの報酬及び貸与奨学金の受給は可”; “支給開始時に、受給が終了…応募できます。”

## Selection process

- Structured summary: First stage is document screening; successful receipt numbers are announced on the Japanese website by 2026-11-05. Second-stage online Japanese interviews are on 2026-11-14 or 2026-11-15 and applicants must be in Japan during the interview. Final successful numbers are announced by 2026-11-20. Successful students must attend the planned in-person certification ceremony in Tokyo on 2026-12-12.
- Official evidence: “一次 書面選考”; “2026年11月14日又は11月15日”; “11月20日まで”; “認証式（12月12日 東京）への出席が必須”

## Renewal or continuation conditions

- Structured summary: No ordinary annual renewal is described within the two-year award. Undergraduate extension to the standard course end applies only where more than two but less than three years remain at adoption. Doctoral continuation beyond two years requires an extension review around 18 months after adoption; failure ends support at two years. Scholars must attend six Foundation exchange meetings per year (mainly in Tokyo) and remain willing to participate as alumni after support ends.
- Official evidence: “延長審査…約18ヶ月後”; “交流会に出席…年6回”; “支援期間終了後も…交流する意思”
- Page title: 私費留学奨学生 募集要項（秋募集）

## Important exceptions

- Structured summary: Reapplication is permitted. If the current scholarship or unrestricted university-program payment ends by the SISF start, the student may apply. A new entrant/transfer applicant without a formal acceptance document cannot apply. Where the destination university cannot act, the Japanese institution attended in July 2026 may be the application window. Foundation-rule violations can suspend payment or terminate scholar status.
- Official evidence: “再応募可”; “受給が終了している場合は応募できます”; “2026年7月時点の在籍校…窓口としての応募も可”
- Page title: 私費留学奨学生 募集要項（秋募集）

## Unconfirmed or missing information

- No numeric GPA, JLPT, EJU, IELTS, or TOEFL threshold is published for the current round.
- The guideline states “15名程度” for the current round but does not disclose course-level allocation or scoring weights.
- University internal deadlines are institution-specific and must be confirmed locally.
- The two document-upload deadlines are labeled A and B in the guideline; applicants must confirm their classification with the university if circumstances do not clearly fit the definitions.
