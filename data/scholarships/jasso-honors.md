# JASSO / MEXT Honors Scholarship for Privately Financed International Students

- Official Japanese name: 留学生受入れ促進プログラム（文部科学省外国人留学生学習奨励費）
- Organization: 独立行政法人日本学生支援機構（JASSO）
- Official URL: https://www.jasso.go.jp/ryugaku/scholarship_j/shoreihi/about.html
- Retrieved date: 2026-08-01
- Source language: Japanese
- Status: active
- Status note: JASSO publishes current 2026 adoption information and states that no institutions are presently under a recruitment suspension. The public page does not state a student application window.

## Overview

- Structured summary: A benefit for privately financed international students enrolled in specified Japanese higher-education, preparatory, or Japanese-language institutions who are academically and personally outstanding and have financial difficulty. Awardees are selected on a university/school recommendation.
- Official evidence: “学業、人物ともに優れ、かつ、経済的理由により修学が困難である者” and “大学又は学校からの推薦により採用者を決定します。”

## Eligible institutions

- Structured summary: Eligible enrollment includes graduate schools; universities; junior colleges; colleges of technology from the third year; professional-training courses at specialized training colleges; advanced courses or international-student special courses at universities, junior colleges, or colleges of technology; designated university-preparatory education institutions; and Japanese-language institutions for students aiming to progress to the listed higher-education categories. Exact enrollment status requirements differ by category.
- Official evidence: “大学院に正規生として在籍” / “高等専門学校第3学年以上” / “準備教育課程を設置する教育機関に正規生として在籍” / “日本語教育機関に在籍”

## Academic requirements

- Structured summary: The previous-year grade coefficient must be at least 2.30 at graduate, undergraduate, and Japanese-language-institution levels, with an expectation that it will be maintained during the award. Where the coefficient cannot be calculated, JASSO permits an equivalent level and recognition of excellent grades.
- Official evidence: “前年度の成績評価係数が…2.30以上であり、給付期間中においてもそれを維持する見込み”
- Page title: 留学生受入れ促進プログラム(文部科学省外国人留学生学習奨励費)について
- Source URL: https://www.jasso.go.jp/ryugaku/scholarship_j/shoreihi/about.html
- Retrieved: 2026-08-01

## Language requirements

- Structured summary: Except for students in 留学生別科, university-preparatory courses, and Japanese-language institutions, the student must meet either Japanese or English criteria: JLPT N2 level or higher; EJU Japanese (reading, listening, and listening-reading comprehension) at least 200; another JASSO-recognized language level; or English CEFR B2 or higher.
- Official evidence: “日本語能力試験N2レベル以上” / “EJU…得点が200点以上” / “CEFRにおいてB2レベル以上”

## Scholarship amount

- Structured summary: Graduate/undergraduate level: JPY 48,000 per month. Japanese-language institutions: JPY 30,000 per month.
- Official evidence: “大学院レベル・学部レベル：月額48,000円” / “日本語教育機関：月額30,000円”

## Payment period

## Restrictions and combination with other scholarships

- Structured summary: Average monthly remittances, excluding admission and tuition fees, must be JPY 90,000 or less. If a supporter resides in Japan, that supporter’s annual income must be under JPY 5,000,000. The student must not receive a scholarship that prohibits combination with this award and must not receive support under JASSO’s Student Exchange Support Program.
- Official evidence: “仕送り…平均月額90,000円以下” / “年収が500万円未満” / “海外留学支援制度による支援を受けている者ではない”
- Page title: 留学生受入れ促進プログラム(文部科学省外国人留学生学習奨励費)について
- Source URL: https://www.jasso.go.jp/ryugaku/scholarship_j/shoreihi/about.html

## Renewal or continuation conditions

- Structured summary: No separate renewal procedure is stated. During the grant, the recipient is expected to maintain the 2.30 grade coefficient and must complete monthly enrollment confirmation. The recipient must also be willing to cooperate with JASSO’s post-award career-status survey through the institution.
- Official evidence: “給付期間中においてもそれを維持する見込み” / “進路状況調査に協力する意思”

## Important exceptions

- Structured summary: Language thresholds do not apply to 留学生別科, preparatory-education courses, or Japanese-language institutions. A graduate-level research student may qualify if they hold at least a bachelor’s degree and are enrolled to conduct graduate-level research. Institutional quotas can make application unavailable even where the student meets the published conditions.
- Official evidence: “留学生別科、準備教育課程、日本語教育機関は除く。” / “研究生として在籍”
- Page title: 留学生受入れ促進プログラム(文部科学省外国人留学生学習奨励費)について
